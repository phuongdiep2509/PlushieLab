Our Story

PlushieLab was born from a simple idea — that a small, soft companion can hold the biggest memories. We craft each plushie with love, care, and a whole lot of cuddle-tested quality.

Little Things, Lasting Memories

Founded in 2022 by a group of plushie-obsessed designers in Vietnam, PlushieLab started as a small workshop experiment. We wanted to create something that felt personal — not just another mass-produced toy, but a handcrafted friend you can truly call your own.

Today, we offer a fully customizable plushie experience — choose your type, color, outfit, accessories, and even have your name embroidered on it. Every plushie leaves our studio wrapped in warmth and ready to make a lasting impression.
